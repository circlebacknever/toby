## Sentence types

1. A statement of a given fact, often two facts joined by "and". About 33 percent. Not grating, except when the two facts have no relation.
   - content-u1.md: "The 43 scripts run on two hosts that nobody owns."
   - content-u3.md: "The queue service retries each failed job 3 times before it sends the page."
   - content-u5.md: "The invoice export was one of the 6 scripts that failed silently last quarter."
2. An instruction in the imperative, in steps, commit bullets, and fix lines. About 15 percent. Not grating.
   - content-u2.md: "Before you open a PR, run `npm run plugins:check`."
   - content-u4.md: "Return a non-200 status from the `catch` block, so the provider retries the event."
   - content-u1.md: "Approve the migration so that phase 1 can start."
3. A cause and its effect joined by "so" or "because". About 15 percent. Grating when a program or an abstract noun is given a reason, and not grating otherwise.
   - content-u5.md: "Your CLAUDE.md already has the Toby marker block, so the installer replaces only the text between the markers."
   - content-u3.md: "The build rejects engine imports because those imports broke every plugin the last time a provider changed its SDK."
   - content-u2.md: "I did not run `evals/run.py gates`, so the gates are not verified."
4. A bullet or sentence that repeats a title, a subject, or an earlier slide, often as arithmetic or as a negative. About 9 percent. Grating.
   - content-u2.md: "$400 a month is $4,800 a year."
   - content-u4.md: "The 3 phases average 2 weeks each."
   - content-u3.md: "Move the 8 jobs from cron to the queue service."
5. A slide title or commit subject written as a whole sentence. About 7 percent. Not grating.
   - content-u1.md: "6 of the 43 cron scripts failed silently last quarter"
   - content-u4.md: "The queue service costs $400 a month."
   - content-u5.md: "The migration runs in 3 phases over 6 weeks, with the highest-risk jobs in phase 1"
6. A short verdict that opens a chat reply. About 7 percent. Grating when it is vague, when it leaves out a condition that changes the answer, or when two replies in a row open with one.
   - content-u3.md: "The install is fixed."
   - content-u2.md: "Keep the validator."

7. A claim about what would have happened, or a consequence of a proposed change. About 5 percent. Grating when it describes last quarter, which nobody can check, and grating when it closes a reply.
